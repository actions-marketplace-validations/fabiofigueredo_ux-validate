# Content evaluation

Read the text **the way the persona would**, considering `reading_level`, `tech_literacy` and `language`.

## Clarity

- The first screen answers: what is this, who is it for, and what can I do here?
- Headings and buttons say the result of the action ("See plans", "Place order"), not vague terms ("Learn more", "Click here", "Submit").
- Short sentences (up to ~20 words for `reading_level: basic`), active voice, one idea per paragraph.
- Technical terms, acronyms and foreign words are avoided or explained.

## Relevance to the persona

- The information the persona is looking for (price, deadline, contact, requirements) is visible without hunting for it.
- The content meets the persona's `expectations`.
- There is no excess of promotional copy hiding the useful information.

## Credibility

- No temporary text, placeholders, lorem ipsum or fake data.
- Numbers, dates, prices and names are consistent across pages.
- Contact information and policies (privacy, returns, cancellation) exist and can be found.
- No claims that look made up (generic testimonials, statistics without a source).

## Tone and language

- A single language, consistent with the persona's `language`.
- A tone suited to the audience (not too formal, no slang that excludes the persona).
- Correct spelling and grammar.

## System messages

- Errors say what happened and what to do, without technical codes.
- Confirmations say what happens next ("You'll get an email within 5 minutes").
- Empty states guide the next step ("You don't have any orders yet. Browse products").
